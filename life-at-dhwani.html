Careers/Life at Dhwani

What it is actually like

Most of our work runs somewhere with patchy connectivity and real consequences for getting it wrong. That shapes the job more than any perk does: you will get to see the people using what you build, and you will be trusted to decide without waiting for permission.

Close to the problem

We build for real field conditions, not a demo environment, so we stay close to the people who use what we ship.

Ownership over instructions

Small teams, real accountability. You are trusted to make the call.

Open, not closed

The same open-source ethos that shapes what we build shapes how we work: transparently, and without unnecessary gatekeeping.

Benefits & policies

Health & leave. Health insurance and a standard leave policy, shared in full during the hiring process.

Learning. Exposure to real engineering and delivery problems, with senior people close by. We use AI in our work, and provide subscriptions where a role needs them, and always for learning. Data confidentiality policies apply to that as they do to everything else.

Hybrid, and flexible. We work a hybrid week out of Gurugram. Where a role allows it, we are flexible about the days and where the work happens.

The specifics. Exact policy documents (leave, insurance, notice periods) are shared with every candidate before you accept an offer, ask us anything at that stage.

The Dhwani Charter

The commitments that decide whether something is ready to leave the building. The full Charter covers how we work with each other too; these are the parts a partner sees.

What we ship

0% regressive bugs

If an error is found it gets documented and added to the automated test suite. The same mistake does not happen twice.

Pixel-perfect UI

The interface is how we show respect for the user. We do not ship clunky or messy. If the UI is broken, trust is broken.

Rural-robust design

Performance is a feature. It has to work on a low-end device at 2G. If it is unusable in a village, it is not Dhwani quality.

How we work with partners

Customer centricity

We do not build for partners, we build with them. Success is how well the tech fits into their system.

The word-to-action ratio

If we commit to a date or a fix, it is done. If something blocks it, you hear it the moment we know, not on the delivery date.

Data sanctity

Beneficiary and client data is handled with real diligence. Their privacy is an absolute right, not a setting.

What to expect

You'll work close to the problem, on software that runs real health, livelihoods, education and governance programmes, often where connectivity is patchy and the stakes are high.

Small teams, real ownership, and the space to do the work properly. Most people get out to the programmes we build for, and see how the work lands.
