Solutions/CloudSahayak

What we do

CloudSahayak is our cloud and DevOps practice for the development sector. Cloud spend in non-profits grows 20–30% a year, and around 60% of a small tech team’s time goes into keeping infrastructure alive. We take that work off your team.

20–30%Annual growth in cloud spend, unchecked
60%Of a small tech team’s time spent keeping infrastructure alive
40+Production workloads we run today
Cloud infrastructure

Multi-cloud design and setup on AWS, Azure, or GCP, multi-account and multi-region management, migration off legacy systems, and cluster design for scaling workloads.

Security, privacy, and compliance

Data protection aligned to DPDP and GDPR, identity and access management with role-based access, VPC hardening, encryption in transit and at rest, and audit-ready infrastructure for donor and grant compliance.

DevOps and automation

CI/CD pipelines for zero-downtime deployment, infrastructure as code in Terraform, CloudFormation, or CDK, and automated patch management with drift prevention.

Cost optimisation and governance

Right-sizing workloads, real-time spend dashboards and alerts, automated clean-up of idle resources, and tagging so every rupee maps to a programme.

Backup and recovery

Fully managed backup for critical data, with recovery plans that are tested rather than assumed.

Monitoring and analytics

24/7 monitoring with automated alerts, dashboards built for donors, boards, and operations teams, and predictive analytics to plan capacity.

GitOps delivery

Declarative infrastructure with Git as the single source of truth, automated application delivery, and rollbacks and audit trails on every change.

Consulting and support

Dedicated cloud engineers who know the sector, hands-on implementation and continuous optimisation, and training so your own team can run it.
