Why Dhwani

Your work ships to the field, not to a backlog.

We build software for NGOs, funders and governments — the systems that run health, livelihoods, education and governance programmes. What you build gets used by real teams, often where connectivity is patchy and the stakes are high.

You will work close to the problem, with people who have spent their careers in the impact sector. Small teams, real ownership, and the space to do the work well.

We hire across engineering, product and delivery, at every level.
