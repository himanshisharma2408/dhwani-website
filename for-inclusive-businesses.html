Who we serve/For Inclusive Businesses

What we build for you

This is not CSR and it is not philanthropy. It is the operating chain of the business, and the people at the far end of it are smallholders, artisans, micro-entrepreneurs or informal workers. Getting that chain to run properly is commercial work that happens to be social work.

Producer and supplier data

One record per farmer, artisan or vendor in your chain, with what they supplied and what they were paid.

Capture at the collection point

Offline-first apps for weighment, quality checks and procurement, on the handset a field officer already carries.

Payments and reconciliation

Payment runs against deliveries, reconciled, so a producer can be told exactly what they are owed and when.

Quality and traceability

Grading, lot-level traceability and quality parameters, from collection centre to retail.

Reporting that satisfies both sides

The commercial numbers your board wants and the inclusion numbers a funder or a regulator asks for, from one system.

Into your existing stack

ERP, accounting and commerce systems, so the inclusive part of the chain is not a spreadsheet on the side.
