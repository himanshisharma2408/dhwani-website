Solutions/AI for Impact

What we build

Chatbots, voice tools, and analytics that run on your own programme data, inside your own systems.

WhatsApp-native chatbots

Beneficiary support, FAQs, service navigation, and data collection on the channel people already have open. 20+ deployments running.

Voice AI and spoken assessments

Speech interfaces in Indian languages, including assessments scored from a spoken answer, for low-literacy and low-connectivity settings.

IVR citizen information

Toll-free interactive voice response with localised content. One of these has been in continuous operation for over a decade.

Document summarisation

Field reports, partner submissions, and proposals turned into a readable brief, with the source paragraph still attached.

Report and content generation

Donor reports, case notes, and programme updates drafted from the data already in your MIS, for a person to edit rather than write from scratch.

Chat with your own data

Ask your MIS a question in plain language and get the number back with the query behind it. MCP-ready, and you can plug in your own API key.

Recommendation engines

Next-best-action for a field worker, or which households a supervisor should visit this week, ranked from your own programme data.

Natural language analytics

The analytics layer over programme data, queried in words instead of filters, for leadership and donors.

How it plugs in

Most of our systems now ship MCP-ready with chat-with-your-data built in. You bring your own API key, so the model provider is your choice and your bill.

On top of your MIS

The AI layer reads the system you already run, whether that is mGrant, mForm, or a platform someone else built.

Your key, your data

Bring your own API key. Data residency and retention follow the same DPDP rules as the rest of your stack.

Multilingual by default

Multilingual natural-language understanding, so a field worker in Odisha and a director in Delhi each ask in their own language.

Radar is our own product built this way: due diligence and risk intelligence on partners and grantees.
